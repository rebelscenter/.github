# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about code, not about people.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith and give direct, specific, technical feedback.
- Accept that other people have context you do not.
- Disagree openly, then commit to the decision that gets made.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing other people's
private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

Applies in all organization repositories, issues, pull requests, and any space
where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to `office@rebels.center`. Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may block accounts, at their
discretion. We will not discuss individual reports publicly.
