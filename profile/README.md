### What we do

**Web applications** — frontends, APIs, backends, dashboards, third-party integrations.

**Web3** — smart contracts, dApps, wallet and chain integrations, on-chain data and indexing.

**Infrastructure** — deployment, CI/CD, monitoring, and the unglamorous work of keeping things running.

### How we work

- **Scope first.** We agree on what gets built, what it costs, and when it lands — before anyone writes code.
- **Ship, don't demo.** Deployed, monitored, documented, handed over.
- **No lock-in.** The code and the infrastructure are yours from day one.
- **Direct contact.** You talk to the engineers, not to an account manager.
