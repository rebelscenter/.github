# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue, pull request, or discussion for security
problems.

Email **security@rebels.center** (or `office@rebels.center`) with:

- what the issue is and which repository or deployment it affects
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- the impact as you see it
- how you would like to be credited, if at all

You can encrypt the report if you prefer — ask us for a key first.

## What to expect

| | |
|---|---|
| First response | within 3 business days |
| Assessment and severity | within 7 business days |
| Fix or mitigation plan | shared with you before disclosure |

We will keep you updated while we work on it, and we will credit you in the
release notes unless you would rather stay anonymous.

## Scope

Repositories under [@rebelscenter](https://github.com/rebelscenter) and services
we operate on `rebels.center` domains. Third-party services we merely use are out
of scope — report those to their owners.

## Please avoid

Denial of service, spam, social engineering of our team or clients, physical
attacks, and any testing that degrades service for other people or touches data
that is not yours.
